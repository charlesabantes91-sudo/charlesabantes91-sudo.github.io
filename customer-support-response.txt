PRACTICE SAMPLE — FICTIONAL CUSTOMER SUPPORT RESPONSE

Customer message:
"Hi, I’d like to check the status of my order."

Suggested response:
"Hello! I’d be happy to help. Could you please share your order number so I can check the latest status for you? Once I have that, I’ll review the details and provide an update. Thank you!"

Demonstrated approach:
- Acknowledge the customer's request.
- Use a polite and helpful tone.
- Ask for the information needed to investigate.
- Explain the next step clearly.
